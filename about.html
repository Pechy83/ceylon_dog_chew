The Beginning

Where the Story Starts

It started with a simple question: why are most commercial dog chews made from synthetic ingredients, shipped thousands of miles, wrapped in plastic — when the world's finest natural chewing material grows right here in our backyard?

Sri Lanka is the home of true Ceylon cinnamon, Cinnamomum verum, a species valued around the world for its distinctive aroma and naturally low coumarin content. Local farmers had been working these plots for generations. All that was missing was someone to turn that raw abundance into something dogs would love — and their owners could feel good about.

That's exactly what we set out to do. In a small workshop surrounded by cinnamon trees, with a handful of craftspeople and a trusted farming family by our side, Ceylon Dog Chew was born.

Our Partnership

More Than Suppliers — Family

Our cinnamon comes from a single extended farming family who have tended these plots organically for three generations. We don't just buy from them — we plan with them, invest with them, and share the rewards with them.

Organic cultivation means no pesticides, no synthetic fertilizers, no shortcuts. The soil stays healthy. The trees stay healthy. And the chews your customers' dogs enjoy are as clean as the island air.

We pay above fair-trade rates, provide equipment, and co-develop processing practices that reduce waste at the source. When you buy Ceylon Dog Chew, you support an entire community, not just a product.

Why We Do This

We're just dog owners who want the best
for our pups
and yours.

01

Caring for the Nature

We only take what nature can easily spare. For us, it’s simple: we want to protect the land so our dogs always have beautiful places to run and explore.

02

Nothing but Cinnamon

We don't like reading complicated labels, so we kept ours short: just pure cinnamon sticks. No hidden chemicals, no artificial nonsense. If we wouldn't give it to our dogs, we won't give it to yours.

03

Wasting Nothing

We hate unnecessary trash. From how we source the wood to the boxes we ship in, we make sure nothing goes to a landfill if it doesn't have to.

04

Love for Dogs

Our dogs are family and we know that yours is too, which is why we personally inspect every single piece of wood before shipping to make sure it's 100% safe for your dog.

05

Naturally Clean Teeth

We love dog kisses, but we can do without the bad breath. As your dog happily chews, the natural wood fibers scrub their teeth like a little toothbrush, keeping their gums healthy and their breath smelling like sweet cinnamon.
