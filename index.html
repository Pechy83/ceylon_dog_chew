Our Story

Born from the Heart of Ceylon's Forests

Nestled in the lush hills of southern Sri Lanka, our small manufactory works hand-in-hand with local farmers to harvest the finest cinnamon bark and native hardwoods — materials that nature itself has perfected over centuries.

Every chew is shaped, dried, and inspected by hand. No chemicals. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just pure, honest craft — and a genuine love for dogs.

Why Ceylon Dog Chew?

Nature Knows Best

Six reasons our wholesale partners across four continents trust us.

🌿
Pure Cinnamon Origin
We use only genuine Ceylon cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) – the highest quality in the world (low coumarin + healthy benefits). Ethically harvested from our farm and partner farms around our workshop in southern Sri Lanka.
♻️
Zero Waste Process
Every part of the harvest is used. Offcuts become compost. Packaging is biodegradable kraft paper. Water from processing is recycled for irrigation.
🐾
Vet-Verified Safe
Our chews contain no artificial binders, flavours, or preservatives. Just cinnamon and love — tested and verified safe for dogs of all sizes.
🤝
Farmer Partnership
We co-own our success with the farming family that supplies us. Fair pay, organic farming practices, and a genuine community relationship.
📦
Bulk B2B Ready
We offer flexible MOQs, custom white-label options, and export logistics tailored for pet-shop distributors, retailers, and eco-brands across EU, USA, Canada and Japan.
🤲
Handcrafted Quality
No two chews are identical — because they're made by human hands, not machines. Each piece carries the texture and character of real Sri Lankan cinnamon.
